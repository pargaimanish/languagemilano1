Meet your teacher

Nishita Chauhan

Nishita has spent close to a decade teaching Spanish to school and college students, working professionals, and hobby learners alike, with a particular focus on students following the IB, CBSE, AP, ICSE, IGCSE and HKDSE curricula. She holds a B.Com from Delhi University and teaches in Hindi, English and Spanish, currently based in Noida, India, and working with students online via Zoom worldwide — including families across time zones preparing for MYP and DP exam sessions.

Her approach centres on pairing language instruction with Spanish culture, so students build genuine comfort with the language rather than memorising it for a single paper.
